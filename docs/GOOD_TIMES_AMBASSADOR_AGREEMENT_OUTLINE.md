# GOOD TIMES — Ambassador Agreement Requirements

This is an operational/legal checklist, not final legal advice. Final contract language should be reviewed by counsel.

Required sections:
1. Parties and effective date
2. Independent ambassador relationship; not employment
3. Term and renewal
4. Role and approved activities
5. GOOD TIMES brand standards
6. Required disclosures and advertising-law compliance
7. Social-media conduct and content standards
8. Referral code / deep-link use
9. No code manipulation, self-referral fraud or unauthorized paid traffic
10. Compensation and campaign-specific reward terms
11. Payout timing and eligibility
12. Returns/refunds/chargeback adjustments where transaction rewards apply
13. Content license and permission to repost approved ambassador content
14. GOOD TIMES trademarks and brand assets
15. Confidential information
16. Event and venue conduct
17. No authority to bind GOOD TIMES
18. Non-exclusivity unless a campaign says otherwise
19. Morals / brand-safety provision drafted narrowly by counsel
20. Termination and immediate code deactivation
21. Post-termination content/code requirements
22. Tax responsibility and required tax forms
23. Dispute / governing-law provisions
24. Electronic signature
