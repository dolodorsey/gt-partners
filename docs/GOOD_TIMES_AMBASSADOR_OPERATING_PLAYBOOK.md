# GOOD TIMES — Ambassador Operating Playbook

## Program purpose
GOOD TIMES ambassadors are local culture connectors who help the brand acquire qualified users, improve local discovery, create credible social proof, and activate Atlanta nightlife, dining, entertainment, sports, fitness and culture.

Current public launch market: **Atlanta only**.

## Operating principle
Ambassador success is measured by attributable behavior, not follower count alone.

Primary funnel:
**Referral click → app install → account created → plan created → reservation/ticket/transaction → repeat activity**

Secondary value:
- Credible local content
- Venue/event discovery
- UGC
- Event attendance
- Community introductions
- Partner/venue leads

## Levels
1. Affiliate
2. Ambassador
3. Core Ambassador
4. City Captain
5. Brand Council

Advancement is performance-based.

## Application score (100 points)
- Brand Fit — 25
- Relationship Strength — 20
- Content Quality — 15
- Engagement / Influence — 15
- Reliability — 10
- Geography — 5
- Professionalism — 5
- Referral Value — 5

Suggested interpretation:
- 85–100: Core priority
- 70–84: Approve
- 55–69: Trial / campaign test
- Under 55: Keep in network; do not make official yet

## Onboarding package
Approved ambassadors receive:
1. Welcome to GOOD TIMES
2. Ambassador Agreement
3. Brand + Voice Guide
4. App Training Guide
5. Content Standards
6. Referral Code + Tracking Guide
7. Campaign Brief Template
8. Compensation / Rewards Guide
9. FTC Disclosure Guide
10. FAQ + Support
11. Monthly Performance Expectations
12. Offboarding / Code Abuse Policy

## Baseline monthly expectations
Default starting requirement:
- 2 Reels or short-form videos
- 4 Stories
- 1 GOOD TIMES app/use-case demonstration
- 1 local recommendation, event, or venue submission
- Use assigned referral link/code
- Attend priority activations when mutually agreed

Requirements can be changed by campaign and ambassador tier.

## Referral codes
GOOD TIMES codes are primarily **attribution codes**, not Shopify-style discount codes.

Track:
- Link clicks
- App installs
- Account creations
- Plans created
- Reservations
- Ticket clicks
- Transactions
- Venue leads
- Content posts
- Event check-ins

Examples:
GOODAMBER
GOODTEASHA
GOODATLJAZ

No code may be shared, transferred, duplicated or manipulated.

## Compensation
Use campaign-based compensation, not one universal rate.

Possible models:
- Product / experience access
- Flat activation fee
- Qualified-action bonus
- Campaign performance bonus
- Revenue share where a measurable transaction exists
- City Captain management bonus
- Venue/partner referral bonus

No compensation is promised until confirmed in writing.

## Monthly HR review
GREEN — active, reliable, compliant, producing measurable value
YELLOW — low activity, coaching needed
RED — nonresponsive, code abuse, brand issue, repeated missed requirements

Actions:
Promote / Maintain / Coach / Pause / Offboard
